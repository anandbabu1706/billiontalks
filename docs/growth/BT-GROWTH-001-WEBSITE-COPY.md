- International English, simple and inviting, distinctive but credible, written for individuals and global teams with priority on startups and small businesses.
- Brand: **Meet Your Team for Free.** / **The meeting doesn't end when the call ends.**
- Official company logo must be loaded from the existing GitHub repository asset; no generated approximation.
- Support useful headings, accessible crawlable text, descriptive links and distinctive information on each page; avoid keyword stuffing, repetitive blocks or image-only content.
- Current V1 is offered free to users. Do not promise permanent free access, unlimited use, verified 100% uptime/HD, encryption certifications, large adoption, recording playback, AI features or file transfer.
- Clearly distinguish existing V1 functionality and future product direction. Confirm release capabilities and URLs against deployed application before publishing.
