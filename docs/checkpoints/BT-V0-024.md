# BT-V0-024 — Bidirectional Realtime Media

Follows BT-V0-023.

## Test 45 Production Evidence

- Host-to-participant video worked, but reconnect could be slow.
- Host-to-participant audio was delayed by approximately 30–60 seconds.
- Participant-to-host video and audio were absent.

The production symptoms required investigation of the complete bidirectional media lifecycle. Simulated PeerConnections cannot prove physical-device behavior; host audio timing and both media directions still require production physical-device validation.

See [BT-V0-024-investigation.md](./BT-V0-024-investigation.md) for the investigation conclusions and validation boundary.

## Corrections

- Split publisher and subscriber client mutations into independent queues, so work or failure in one direction does not block the other.
- Kept recovery serialized and bounded to three attempts per direction, resetting the budget after 30 seconds of stable connectivity.
- Sequenced publisher readiness after Cloudflare accepts the publish and the browser applies the answer. Readiness includes a publisher-generation check to reject stale sessions.
- Bounded ICE gathering and allowed candidate fallback only when the local SDP contains candidates.
- Persisted only tracks with identifiable successful per-track Cloudflare results.
- Corrected publication-discovery cache timing: a failed negotiation does not cache the set; valid no-op responses and completed negotiations do. Unchanged successful sets therefore do not issue repeated mutations.
- Preserved remote streams and playback elements through subscriber recovery while removing stale track mappings before stopping old tracks.
- Added lifecycle diagnostics and redacted RTP statistics. Diagnostics contain aggregate direction/media counters and omit raw transport IDs, SSRCs, track identifiers, and addresses.

## Regression Coverage

Coverage includes independent publisher/subscriber progress, bounded recovery, publisher generation checks, per-track publish acceptance, ICE timeout/candidate fallback, failed-negotiation retry, publication-cache behavior, and subscriber recovery without duplicate or discarded media elements. Server coverage verifies host and admitted-guest microphone/camera publications, guest readiness and recovery, identity and snapshot stability, and host discovery of ready guest tracks.

These tests use simulated PeerConnections and mocked SFU responses. They cannot establish actual physical-device exchange or timing.

## Remaining Production Validation

On physical devices, confirm host and participant audio/video in both directions, verify microphone and camera tracks are ready and discoverable, measure host audio timing, and exercise reconnects. This validation has not been performed.
