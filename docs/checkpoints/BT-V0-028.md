# BT-V0-028 - Media Toggle Isolation and Resume Video Recovery

Follows BT-V0-027. Real-device validation showed initial media, playback and recovery working, with four remaining defects.

## Production Observations

- Muting the microphone (host or guest) could freeze video.
- Backgrounding a mobile browser froze that participant's video while audio continued; restoration could take up to ~30 s.
- Raw transport text such as `Cloudflare Realtime did not return a subscription offer.` appeared in the meeting UI.

## Mic Mute Root Cause

Mute was a hard stop: `stopLocalMicrophone()` closed the mic transceiver, sent `/media/tracks/close` with a new offer and stopped the capture track. Unmute re-captured the mic and republished it (`/media/publish` and `/media/publish/ready`). The publication set changed on the server, so remote subscribers saw a removed then added publication and renegotiated. That subscriber churn froze video. The publisher PeerConnection and camera transceiver were not touched directly.

## Changes (src/index.ts)

- **Soft mic mute:** `state.localDevice.micMuted` toggles `track.enabled`. No track stop, close, offer, republish, recovery or subscriber rebuild. Camera toggling is unchanged.
- **Persistence:** the muted state is re-applied to reused or reacquired mic tracks and survives resume. It resets only when the mic is stopped.
- **Recording mixer:** disabled audio tracks are skipped (the only recording-related change).
- **Video-only resume health checks:** on foreground, publisher `outbound-rtp` and subscriber `inbound-rtp` bytes/frames are sampled (`resumeRtpProbeMs` = 700) and audio and video judged independently.
- **Publisher video repair ladder (bounded):** `replaceTrack` on the existing camera sender, then camera-only republish, then the existing bounded publisher recovery. Unmeasurable stats fall back to existing recovery.
- **Ended camera:** reacquired and swapped into the existing sender.
- **Subscriber:** a video stall with flowing audio forces publication rediscovery and a re-probe before any rebuild.
- **Concurrency:** publisher and subscriber resume checks run concurrently.
- **Healthy audio preserved** during video-only recovery; healthy resume no longer rebuilds the publisher.
- **Resume timing epoch** is no longer overwritten when publisher recovery starts.
- **Error masking:** `Cloudflare`, `Realtime`, `did not return`, `session_error` and `errorCode` are masked to `Reconnecting media…`; exact text stays in the console. A terminal rejoin message appears only after bounded recovery fails.
- **Retry:** `did not return a subscription offer` is retryable with the same bounded rediscovery then single rebuild as `did not accept any remote tracks`.
- **Diagnostics:** `btMediaDiagnostics().videoHealth` holds timestamp, trigger, classification, actions, publisher/subscriber audio/video RTP verdicts and a remote playback snapshot.

## Remaining ~30 s Path

Likely the full publisher rebuild followed by the subscriber rebuild, each exposed to the 8 s connected wait or 10 s request timeout, plus retry backoffs and 1 s/3 s polling. The unnecessary rebuilds are removed; final timing needs real-device data.

## Tests

238 tests pass (229 before). New "BT-V0-028" block covers:
- host mic mute/unmute isolation
- guest mic mute/unmute isolation (no close, no new offer, no republish, no subscriber rebuild)
- mute persistence across resume
- healthy resume without rebuild
- `replaceTrack` repair
- ended camera reacquisition
- bounded repair ladder
- subscriber rediscovery before rebuild
- raw subscription-offer error masking

Three existing tests that encoded the hard-stop mute behavior were updated.

## Remaining Real-Device Validation

- Mic mute/unmute without video freeze, host and guest.
- Mobile background/foreground video restoration (browser may leave capture `muted` or `ended`; stats availability after resume).
- Actual recovery timing.
- Camera frames pause while genuinely backgrounded, which is an OS/browser limit.
