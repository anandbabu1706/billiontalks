# BJ-V0-002: Architecture & Integration Design

**PROPOSED architecture baseline.** The design and independent review were delivered; owner confirmations for separate Jobs D1 and company permissions are recorded individually in the [decision register](BJ-DECISION-REGISTER.md). Neither approval authorizes provisioning, deployment, or implementation. Evidence IDs E1–E4 are in the [audit](BJ-V0-001-ARCHITECTURE-AUDIT.md#verified-repository-evidence).

## Module boundaries and extraction

| Status | Boundary | Ownership and reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| APPROVED | Account reuse | Existing BillionTalks identity owns credentials, sessions and recovery. Jobs references stable authenticated subject IDs and owns consent and product profile data. No copied passwords or sessions. E3. |
| PROPOSED | Jobs routing/runtime | Use same-host `/jobs` and `/api/jobs/v1`, with Jobs off by default and existing routes unchanged. Start with a modular TypeScript Worker in the existing repository for synthetic staging qualification. E3 host-only session handling and E4 existing toolchain support this. |
| APPROVED | Separate Jobs D1 and company roles | Jobs owns candidates, profiles, companies, memberships, listings, applications, interview links, offer records and Jobs entitlements. No cross-product database queries or cross-database foreign keys. Separate per-environment bindings and distinguish test fixtures from real data. Resource creation still needs approval. |
| PROPOSED | Meetings adapter | Meetings keeps its schedules, invitations/admission, Durable Objects, media, and recording storage. Jobs uses a minimal versioned server-side adapter, with opaque cross-product references and no direct access to room objects or recordings. E2 and E4. |
| PROPOSED | Notifications, storage, billing | In-app notification first; optional approved email adapter after qualification. Send generic alerts without application or offer terms. No initial résumé bucket, object uploads or payment data. Later private Jobs R2 only after validation, scanning/quarantine, access expiry, deletion and cost review. Entitlements can be modeled without collecting money or handling cards. E4 and approved deferrals. |

**Future interface design gate (PROPOSED):** Before taking an extension into development, identify provider and consumer, authoritative data owner, least-privilege API scope, version/compatibility policy, failure and rollback contract, and measurable extraction triggers. Document these requirements at the point of need without building speculative interfaces.

**PROPOSED extraction design:** Put each module behind an owned API and repository; do not share business tables, feature internals or privileged bindings. Record dependency direction, service interface version, failure semantics, ownership, rollout and rollback before extracting. A separate D1 is a data boundary, not runtime isolation. Move Jobs to its own Worker with narrow service bindings if threat modelling, fault isolation, release cadence, compliance or measured contention justifies it; reevaluate before live/public launch. Preserve existing routes and dependent callers when moving. Do not extract simply to prepare for hypothetical scale. See the [extension register](BJ-DECISION-REGISTER.md#extension--integration-register).

## Identity, organization authorization and privacy

**PROPOSED:** Check the signed-in verified account on the server, deny by default, and load current Jobs membership and object permissions on each sensitive operation. Candidates own their profiles and applications; company recruiters see only records in their company and assigned openings; company administrators manage membership, not implicit applicant exports. Designate offer issuer permission separately. Employers control internal authorizations and hiring policy; BillionJobs checks only the authenticated representative's platform issuer permission, without approving or reviewing internal HR decisions. Audit narrowly scoped moderator access and sensitive actions; refresh or revoke promptly, require step-up for high-impact actions before real data, and prevent enumeration. Tenant/subject scopes come from verified context, not client selections. Cover mutations with origin/CSRF protections, validation, replay/duplicate controls and rate limits. Keep private profile defaults until visibility policy is approved.

**PROPOSED verification standards:** Apply relevant [OWASP ASVS](https://owasp.org/www-project-application-security-verification-standard/), [OWASP API Security guidance](https://owasp.org/www-project-api-security/), [NIST SSDF](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/218/final), and [WCAG 2.2 AA](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) checks proportionately, without claiming certification. Minimize collection and redact tokens, contact details, applicant content and offer terms from logs. **REQUIRES VERIFICATION:** Retention, deletion/export, access review, region, recovery, monitoring and incident ownership before processing real applicant records.

## Proposed Jobs-to-Meetings contract

- Jobs initiates create, reschedule and cancel only for an authorized ATS record. Send an opaque correlation reference, stable host and invitee references, UTC times with IANA time zone for display, and a neutral title. No résumé, salary or application fields.
- Meetings owns schedule/admission and returns an opaque schedule ID, revision, state and authorized join reference. Use idempotency keys for retries, revision checks for concurrent changes, explicit conflicts and reconciliation for ambiguous timeouts. Revalidate authorization before delayed deliveries; bounded retry, deduplication and operator recovery after repeated failure.
- A copied link is not access authority. Only intended participants with current entitlement may enter; a cancelled or superseded invitation must not remain usable. Keep interview functionality off until cross-tenant denials, join access, no-show timing, reschedule/cancel, time-zone and notification behavior are qualified. Recording is not required for this integration.

## Decision trade-offs, costs and review gates

| Status | Decision | Trade-off and required evidence |
|---|---|---|
| PROPOSED | Same-host modular runtime versus immediate new Worker | Reuses E3/E4 and minimizes setup. Shared blast radius requires route compatibility, flag rollback and regression evidence; revisit with a fresh threat model before live use. |
| APPROVED | Separate Jobs data versus shared product tables | Limits data coupling; additional binding, migrations and recovery work will need explicit authorization. Test cross-tenant isolation even with separate D1. |
| PROPOSED | Controlled API adapter versus direct access to Meetings tables and DOs | Protects service ownership at the cost of explicit conflict/retry handling. Verify active deployment/API on the chosen build baseline. |
| PROPOSED | In-app/no file/no checkout first | Limits operational burden and sensitive data. Keep interfaces extensible without adding an email service, attachment storage or a payment vendor. |
| REQUIRES VERIFICATION | Incremental cash and capacity | ₹0 target, not a guarantee. Illustrative load: 500 sessions × 12 calls = 6,000 calls/day, × 30 rows read = 180,000 rows/day; 100 applications × 6 writes = 600 writes/day before index and other writes. At 10×: 60,000 calls, 1.8M reads and 6,000 writes/day under these assumptions, excluding existing traffic and bursts. Compare actual combined peaks and budgets to [Workers](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/limits/), [D1](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/platform/pricing/) and [R2](https://developers.cloudflare.com/r2/pricing/) limits; include DO, Realtime, email and storage usage. Verify mandatory costs separately. No incremental paid purchase identified as necessary for synthetic design work; operating expenses are unknown. |
| PROPOSED | Operating guardrails | Separate aggregate Jobs and Meetings metrics; record error/latency percentiles, D1 row counts, queue/backlog and join failures without logging applicant details. Investigate approaching 70% of verified shared quota as a planning trigger, not a provider limit. |

**Independent review verdict (PROPOSED):** Suitable for owner planning review, not a go-live recommendation. Qualification must show two-tenant access tests, revocation and deletion behavior, accessible keyboard/focus/error journeys, Meetings regression and reversible rollout, real environment bindings and budget headroom, and feature-specific interview and offer safeguards. Run synthetic staged checks only after authorization on the agreed branch. No deployment or production testing is part of this document.
