# BJ-V0-002: Architecture & Integration Design

**BJ-V0-002A: CONDITIONALLY APPROVED design direction.** BJ-PLAN-001, BJ-D26 and REV-004/REV-005 approve the approach subject to demonstrated security isolation and Codex technical verification during separately authorized implementation. See the [Decision Register](BJ-DECISION-REGISTER.md) and [audit evidence E1–E4](BJ-V0-001-ARCHITECTURE-AUDIT.md#verified-repository-evidence). No build, provisioning or deployment authorized.

## Module boundaries and extraction

| Status | Boundary | Ownership and reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| APPROVED | Account reuse | Existing BillionTalks identity owns credentials, sessions and recovery. Jobs references stable authenticated subject IDs and owns consent and product profile data. No copied passwords or sessions. E3. |
| CONDITIONALLY APPROVED | Jobs routing/runtime | Use same-host `/jobs` and `/api/jobs/v1`, with Jobs off by default and existing routes unchanged. Start with a modular TypeScript Worker in the existing repository for synthetic staging qualification only, pending authorization. E3 host-only session handling and E4 existing toolchain support this. |
| APPROVED | Separate Jobs D1 and company roles | Jobs owns candidates, profiles, companies, memberships, listings, applications, interview links, self-reported recruitment status events and Jobs entitlements. Offer-letter issuance, storage, distribution and verification are deferred under BJ-D17. No cross-product database queries or cross-database foreign keys. Separate per-environment bindings and distinguish test fixtures from real data. Resource creation still needs approval. |
| CONDITIONALLY APPROVED | Meetings adapter | Meetings keeps its schedules, invitations/admission, Durable Objects, media, and recording storage. Jobs uses a minimal versioned server-side adapter, with opaque cross-product references and no direct access to room objects or recordings. E2 and E4. |
| APPROVED (planning scope) | Notifications, storage, billing | In-app notification first; optional approved email adapter after qualification. Send generic alerts without application or offer terms. No initial résumé bucket, object uploads or payment data. Later private Jobs R2 only after validation, scanning/quarantine, access expiry, deletion and cost review. Entitlements can be modeled without collecting money or handling cards. E4 and approved deferrals. |

**Future interface design gate (PROPOSED):** Before taking an extension into development, identify provider and consumer, authoritative data owner, least-privilege API scope, version/compatibility policy, failure and rollback contract, and measurable extraction triggers. Document these requirements at the point of need without building speculative interfaces.

**PROPOSED extraction design:** Put each module behind an owned API and repository; do not share business tables, feature internals or privileged bindings. Record dependency direction, service interface version, failure semantics, ownership, rollout and rollback before extracting. A separate D1 is a data boundary, not runtime isolation. Reuse infrastructure only where adequate security isolation is demonstrably established (REV-004). If Jobs can reach privileged Meetings bindings or meaningful release/failure isolation cannot be proven, require a separate Jobs runtime with narrow service interfaces before real data. Verify staging/production binding separation and independently switchable rollout and rollback. No new runtime is approved by this design. Preserve existing routes and dependent callers when moving. Do not extract simply to prepare for hypothetical scale. See the [extension register](BJ-DECISION-REGISTER.md#extension--integration-register).

## Identity, organization authorization and privacy

**APPROVED design, enforcement pending:** Check the signed-in verified account on the server, deny by default, and load current Jobs membership and object permissions on each sensitive operation. Candidates own their profiles and applications; company recruiters see only records in their company and assigned openings; company administrators manage membership, not implicit applicant exports. Enforce employer- and candidate-reported status actions independently; permit candidate withdrawal of acceptance or non-joining as a new audited event (REV-001). Do not introduce offer-document or issuer permissions in MVP. Audit narrowly scoped moderator access and sensitive actions; refresh or revoke promptly, require step-up for high-impact actions before real data, and prevent enumeration. Tenant/subject scopes come from verified context, not client selections. Cover mutations with origin/CSRF protections, validation, replay/duplicate controls and rate limits. Enforce BJ-D18: PRIVATE default, optional RECRUITERS_ONLY and PUBLIC field-scoped projections, independent indexing opt-in, and explicit application disclosure. Apply denial and field selection across reads, exports, notifications and lists; never public-cache authenticated responses. Test purge and visibility changes independently of indexing.

**Approved verification standards, execution pending:** Apply relevant [OWASP ASVS](https://owasp.org/www-project-application-security-verification-standard/), [OWASP API Security guidance](https://owasp.org/www-project-api-security/), [NIST SSDF](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/218/final), and [WCAG 2.2 AA](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) checks proportionately, without claiming certification. Minimize collection and redact tokens, contact details, applicant content and offer terms from logs. **REQUIRES VERIFICATION:** Retention, deletion/export, access review, region, recovery, monitoring and incident ownership before processing real applicant records.

## Conditionally approved Jobs-to-Meetings contract

- Jobs initiates create, reschedule and cancel only for an authorized ATS record. Send an opaque correlation reference, stable host and invitee references, UTC times with IANA time zone for display, and a neutral title. No résumé, salary or application fields.
- Meetings owns schedule/admission and returns an opaque schedule ID, revision, state and authorized join reference. Use idempotency keys for retries, revision checks for concurrent changes, explicit conflicts and reconciliation for ambiguous timeouts. Revalidate authorization before delayed deliveries; bounded retry, deduplication and operator recovery after repeated failure.
- A copied link is not access authority. Only intended participants with current entitlement may enter; a cancelled or superseded invitation must not remain usable. Keep interview functionality off until cross-tenant denials, join access, no-show timing, reschedule/cancel, time-zone and notification behavior are qualified. Recording is not required. Stop issuing Jobs join links immediately when cancellation is pending; reconcile with read-back and require authoritative Meetings denial for stale or cancelled access. Qualify independently of ordinary recruiting.

## Atomic posting and application ledger (BJ-D20/BJ-D21/BJ-D26)

Enforce 10 shared company actions per IST day for creation, renewal and reopening, 30 simultaneously active jobs, 30-day validity, duplicate prevention and non-counting edits. Store authoritative UTC, derive the IST date on the server, and use status/expiry rather than background cleanup to count active jobs. Active renewal adds 30 days to its prior expiry; expired renewal or reopen begins 30 days at successful action (REV-002). Reject beyond either cap and preserve allowable archive history.

Use a single verified atomic operation for membership, active/duplicate checks, successful listing mutation and quota consumption. Apply one-active-application uniqueness, scoped idempotency and atomic state transitions; do not consume quota when a conditional write affects zero records. **REV-005:** During separately authorized implementation, have Codex verify actual Cloudflare D1 primitives and deployed-environment behavior. A [documented D1 batch](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/worker-api/d1-database/#batch) is a candidate, not proof of conventional interactive transactions or safe conditional/concurrent enforcement. Test 10th/11th, 30th/31st, concurrent publish/renew/reopen, IST midnight (18:30 UTC prior day), rollback/no partial spend, duplicate and retry. Stop and review a safer design if verification fails. No new durability service is assumed.

## Decision trade-offs, costs and review gates

| Status | Decision | Trade-off and required evidence |
|---|---|---|
| CONDITIONALLY APPROVED | Same-host modular runtime versus immediate new Worker | Reuses E3/E4 and minimizes setup. Shared blast radius requires route compatibility, flag rollback and regression evidence; revisit with a fresh threat model before live use. |
| APPROVED | Separate Jobs data versus shared product tables | Limits data coupling; additional binding, migrations and recovery work will need explicit authorization. Test cross-tenant isolation even with separate D1. |
| CONDITIONALLY APPROVED | Controlled API adapter versus direct access to Meetings tables and DOs | Protects service ownership at the cost of explicit conflict/retry handling. Verify active deployment/API on the chosen build baseline. |
| APPROVED (planning) | In-app/no file/no checkout first | Limits operational burden and sensitive data. Keep interfaces extensible without adding an email service, attachment storage or a payment vendor. |
| REQUIRES VERIFICATION | Incremental cash and capacity | ₹0 target, not a guarantee. Illustrative load: 500 sessions × 12 calls = 6,000 calls/day, × 30 rows read = 180,000 rows/day; 100 applications × 6 writes = 600 writes/day before index and other writes. At 10×: 60,000 calls, 1.8M reads and 6,000 writes/day under these assumptions, excluding existing traffic and bursts. Compare actual combined peaks and budgets to [Workers](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/limits/), [D1](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/platform/pricing/) and [R2](https://developers.cloudflare.com/r2/pricing/) limits; include DO, Realtime, email and storage usage. Before approving additional expenditure, verify actual usage, existing subscriptions, remaining capacity and billing (REV-006). Verify mandatory costs separately. No incremental paid purchase identified as necessary for synthetic design work; operating expenses are unknown. |
| APPROVED (planning) | Operating guardrails | Separate aggregate Jobs and Meetings metrics; record error/latency percentiles, D1 row counts, queue/backlog and join failures without logging applicant details. Investigate approaching 70% of verified shared quota as a planning trigger, not a provider limit. |

**Independent review verdict:** Conditionally approved planning baseline, not a go-live recommendation. Start with synthetic data only after authorization. Prove tenant separation and runtime isolation, concurrency outcomes, revocation/deletion, accessible workflows, verified live binding/billing evidence, Jobs rollback and Meetings regression. Add invite-only pilot only as a controlled test after separate approval (REV-007). No testing or implementation completion claimed.
